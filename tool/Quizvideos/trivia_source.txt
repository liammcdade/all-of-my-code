Christmas Trivia Facts:

Movies & TV:
- The movie "Die Hard" was released in 1988 and is often debated as a Christmas movie.
- In the movie "Home Alone", Kevin's family is flying to Paris when they leave him behind.
- The animated special "Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer" was first broadcast in 1964.
- "A Christmas Story" features a major subplot about a Red Ryder BB gun.
- The Grinch is a green character who lives on Mount Crumpit.
- In "Elf", Will Ferrell's character is named Buddy.

Songs & Music:
- "Jingle Bells" was originally written for Thanksgiving in 1857 by James Lord Pierpont.
- "Silent Night" was first performed in 1818 in the Austrian village of Oberndorf.
- Mariah Carey's "All I Want for Christmas Is You" was released in 1994.
- The 12 Days of Christmas gifts include partridges, turtle doves, and French hens.
- Brenda Lee's "Rockin' Around the Christmas Tree" was recorded when she was just 13 years old.

Traditions & History:
- The tradition of kissing under the mistletoe dates back to ancient Norse mythology.
- Santa Claus is based on Saint Nicholas, a 4th-century Greek bishop.
- The tallest Christmas tree ever displayed was a 221-foot Douglas fir in Seattle in 1950.
- In Japan, it is a popular tradition to eat Kentucky Fried Chicken (KFC) for Christmas dinner.
- The first recorded Christmas tree was decorated in 1510 in Riga, Latvia.
- Candy canes were originally made to keep children quiet in church during the 17th century.
- The poinsettia plant is native to Mexico and was introduced to the US by Joel Roberts Poinsett.
- The traditional Christmas colors of red and green come from holly berries and evergreen trees.
- Stockings were originally hung by the fireplace to dry.
